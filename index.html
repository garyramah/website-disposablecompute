02 / THE DOCTRINE

Trust is not claimed.
It is engineered.

Disappear into the day

No feed. No endless scroll. No interface demanding a relationship. The best interface is the one you stop noticing.

Earn the room

An ambient device enters intimate territory. Its behavior should be legible, its state visible and its silence deliberate.

Keep the boundary close

The Acolyte is being engineered around a local-first posture: minimize what leaves the device and make every escalation explicit.

Act, then get out of the way

Capture the thought. Find the next step. Clear the small task. Then return the human to the human moment.

03 / THE SYSTEM

A dedicated intelligence.
With rules.

AGENCY ENVELOPE / CONCEPTSENSE → INTERPRET → CONTEXT → ACT

Ambient intelligence should remain observant without becoming performative. The Acolyte is designed to be available, not loud.

It prefers local context, local control and minimal leakage. When something must leave the room, it should be explicit and deliberate.

Capability is not permission. The device should be designed to understand limits before it is asked to expand them.

We are designing the system as a whole rather than layering intelligence onto a generic appliance. Design and behavior must reinforce one another.

04 / THE TRUST WOUND

Your life is not inventory.

THE STRUCTURAL QUESTIONWhen a device can hear the room, the question is not how polished its promise sounds. The question is who the intelligence ultimately serves.

THE ACOLYTE ANSWERA device should make its boundaries obvious, minimize unnecessary exposure and remain subordinate to the person who owns it.
